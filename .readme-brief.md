- Problem (confirmed): You had no record of which models you could use, through which harness, or how good each one was at what.
- Occasion (confirmed): A new model comes out, you want to drop a model, a rating feels wrong, or you want a new profile, such as low cost, speed or higher intelligence preferred.
- Incumbent (confirmed): You switched models by hand, edited every agent, skill or script that named a model, or stuck with the harness's default, and judged each model in your head with nothing written down.
- Value (confirmed): You change one file, and every agent and app that routes through it follows. Your ratings live in that file instead of your head, so routing follows your judgment and a wrong rating is one edit away. You switch between priorities like low cost, speed or intelligence by choosing a profile, without touching an agent, and every model and harness you can reach is listed in one place.
- Non-goals (confirmed): It isn't a router; model-router ranks routes from it. It doesn't rate models or fetch benchmarks for you; it ships prompts that walk an agent through rating, using your sources and with your approval. It isn't a model catalog; it lists only what you add. If you use one model, you don't need it.
